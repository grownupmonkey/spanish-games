# 20-3Y final: Las siete ciudades de oro (s20-9)

- Game: https://claude.ai/artifact/87LwUa78nQXpZrLEvBVTu3 · report game id 29 (in `GAMES`, parts/report.js).
- Source: `scenes/s20-9.html`, `scenes/s20-9.css`, `scenes/s20-9/00…07-*.js` (one scene split into numbered files). The updated build script is `build.py.txt` (rename to build.py): it joins a scene folder, lists s20-9 in `SCENES`, and adds `<!DOCTYPE html>` + a viewport meta to built pages. Tests: `tests/shots9.py.txt` (full playthrough with screenshots; each puzzle sets `window.__pzSolve()`), `tests/gallery9.py.txt` (every city scene).
- Design: a Carmen Sandiego-style chase built on the Cíbola legend. Elena Robles (born Granada 1890, map-maker) left 7 seals; a thief (one of 5 suspects with 4 features; the first 3 clues are never enough) is one city ahead. Mexico City and Miami are always on the route, plus 5 of 9 at random (Bogotá, San Juan, Oaxaca, Madrid, Buenos Aires, Cusco, Antigua, Puerto Viejo, Havana), then Granada. Everything comes from a 20-bit seed (`makeGame`).
- In each city: 3 witnesses (−1 h each; witnesses 0 and 1 describe the next city, and one clue may be a Dato from an earlier game; witness 2 describes the thief), 2 new Dato sparkles, a seal puzzle (glyph: Sun Stone · route: Cabeza de Vaca, Flota de Indias · timeline: San Juan, Puerto Viejo · pick: balsa, mole, colectivo · grid: Madrid, Antigua · quipu: Cusco), Elena's letter, an Archivo card (short comprehensible input + long Spanish text + English, with a year), and a ¿Te acuerdas? question (+2 h) in familiar cities.
- Clock: Monday 08:00 to Sunday 08:00, with nights slept. Total flight time is normalised to 68 h per route. Perfect play leaves about 37 h; two wrong flights leave about 23 h.
- Granada: name the thief → refute four claims with evidence cards (3 hearts) → place the seals by year → final letter (the seven cities of gold are Elena's family; the eighth is Lethbridge; each city's second witness is a cousin) → three.js r128 ending with narration (2D fallback).
- Resume code (16 characters: seed, stop, clock, stars, counters) shown on arrival in each city.
