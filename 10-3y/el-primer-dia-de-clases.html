Para el profe: how this scene works
  • The school is a 3D hallway (three.js). Students walk with the arrow keys or the on-screen buttons, read the number or picture sign above each door, and press Entrar at the door. A small map in the corner shows where they are. If a laptop can’t run 3D, the scene falls back to the clickable 2D map. Students pick a girl (styled like Lucía) or boy avatar at the start.
  • Each game builds a new timetable from seven subjects, with new classroom numbers. The bell gives the time in words (Son las nueve menos cinco) while the timetable uses digits, so students convert between the two to find the room.
  • Matemáticas is a number dictation (10–99) by ear. Educación física is a coach giving ustedes commands (Salten, Corran, Toquen los pies). Other classes use desk commands (Saquen el cuaderno, Abran el libro, Guarden las tijeras, Levanten la mano): students pick the object, then the matching verb.
  • At recess, students write about their favourite class with porque. The checker looks for a subject and a reason, and nudges masculine adjectives about la clase (divertido → divertida).
  • A first wrong room gets “mira tu horario”; a second gets the full answer and the room glows. Mistakes and hints are counted per period.
  • Outcomes practised: LC–1.3 a (school facilities, classroom, time and calendar), A–3.1 c (respond to simple instructions), LC–2.1 a, LC–3.1 a, LC–3.2 a, A–2.1 a (express preferences), LC–4.4 b (classroom expressions).
  • Audio uses one Spanish voice from this computer: checking…. Microsoft Edge usually offers natural-sounding Microsoft voices. If it says “none found”, the audio buttons are hidden and the scene works with text only.
