¡Fallas!

It’s March in Valencia, Spain, and the city is celebrating las Fallas: giant sculptures, fireworks, flowers and fire. You’ll spend the week with the family of Lucía’s friend Nerea.

  • El plan. Listen to the family’s voice messages and plan the week so it works for everyone. Then tell them the plan in the simple future.
  • La semana. Live each day in 3D: find people, vote for a ninot, carry your flowers, order food, and watch the cremà. Drag to look around; click the sparkles to find six Datos de Valencia.
  • El blog. Write about your week in the preterite.

Everything is spoken. Text is off: you can turn it on in ⚙ Ajustes, and your report will say so.

        Para el profe
        • How it works. Three parts. El plan: five family voice messages each give one rule (a busy afternoon, a fixed day for the ofrenda, buñuelos only in the afternoon…). Students place four activities in a 3-day grid; every activity also has a real-world rule (the mascletà is at 2 p.m., the Ofrenda is on 17–18 March, the lights are after dark). Then they write the plan to the family in the simple future. La semana: each planned activity becomes a 3D scene with a listening task: find the cousins from a spoken location, vote for the ninot the president describes (object + clothing colour, with a decoy that shares the object), pick the bouquet Amparo describes (flowers + ribbon, with decoys that share one feature), order buñuelos for three people with usted and pick the total the vendor says, and answer two questions about the cremà announcement. El blog: a preterite post about the week, checked against what actually happened in that playthrough.
        • Randomized every time. The family’s rules (always solvable, 1–4 valid plans), so the plan differs by student; the falla themes (five themes, random riders and figures); the cousins’ meeting point; the ninot, its decoy and colours; the bouquets; the three food orders and so the total; the cremà instruction.
        • Grammar checked (LC–1.4 b, structured). Simple future in the plan message: regular and irregular stems (haremos, saldremos, tendremos, podremos, vendremos, pondremos, diremos), with corrections for haceremos, saliremos, teneremos…; ir a + infinitive and the present are accepted but nudged toward the future. Preterite in the blog: regular, spelling-change (llegué, busqué, empecé), stem-change (pidió, durmió) and irregular forms (fuimos, hicimos, tuvimos, vimos), with corrections for fuemos, hacimos, estuvemos… and future forms used for past events. Days are checked against the student’s own week.
        • Modelled, never marked. The future in every family message, the preterite in reactions, usted with the vendor (noted, not marked: LC–4.1 b), vosotros forms from a Spanish family, valenciano phrases (the mascletà signal, signs).
        • Stars. Plan: by wrong checks. Message: all four activities with the right day and a future verb (3★ with 2+ different verbs and no slips). Week: by listening slips across all five scenes. Blog: 5+ preterite verbs (3+ different), 4+ events with the right days, five sentences (3★ with a personal detail and at most one slip).
        • Accuracy notes. Mascletà daily 1–19 March at 2 p.m. in the Plaza del Ayuntamiento, started by the fallera mayor in valenciano; the Ofrenda on 17–18 March builds the Virgin’s cloak of carnations on a wooden frame in the Plaza de la Virgen; the cremà is the night of 19 March, the town-square falla last (about 1 a.m.); the ninot indultat is chosen by public vote. Fallas themes and the neighbourhood (“Falla Carrer del Sol”) are invented.
